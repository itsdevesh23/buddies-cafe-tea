# ☕ Buddies Cafe: Discovery & Planning Meeting

Use this checklist during your meeting with the cafe owner to gather all the business logic and assets required to take the prototype to a fully production-ready state.

## 1. Product & Inventory (Sanity CMS)
We need to ensure the catalog matches reality.
- [ ] **Catalog Accuracy**: Are the 21 currently listed teas and kombuchas the complete menu?
- [ ] **Pricing**: Are the prices (₹399, ₹249, etc.) accurate for online sales? Do prices include GST, or should GST be calculated at checkout?
- [ ] **High-Quality Photography**: We need actual, high-resolution photos of the tea packaging and the physical cafe for the website. Does he have a professional photoshoot planned, or do we need to organize one?
- [ ] **Stock Tracking**: Does he want online stock to sync with his physical store stock, or will he manually manage a separate "online-only" inventory in Sanity?

## 2. Operations & Logistics
How will the physical tea get to the customer?
- [ ] **Shipping Zones**: Does he want to ship Pan-India, or only locally (e.g., within the city/state)?
- [ ] **Delivery Charges**: Should we charge a flat shipping rate (e.g., ₹50), or offer Free Shipping on orders over a certain amount (e.g., Free over ₹999)?
- [ ] **In-Store Pickup**: Does he want an option at checkout for customers to order online and "Pick up at Cafe" for free?

## 3. Payments & Legal
To hook up real money, we need his business details.
- [ ] **Payment Gateway**: We plan to use Razorpay or Cashfree. We will need his Business PAN, GSTIN (if applicable), and bank account details to create the merchant account.
- [ ] **Legal Pages**: Payment gateways strictly require us to have a *Refund Policy*, *Terms of Service*, and *Privacy Policy* visible on the site. Does he have these, or should we draft standard templates for him to approve?

## 4. The Admin Dashboard
We are building a custom admin portal. What features are a priority for him?
- [ ] **Order Management**: He will need a screen to view new orders and mark them as "Packed" or "Shipped" (which will update the customer's tracking timeline).
- [ ] **Promo Codes**: Does he want the ability to generate custom discount codes (e.g., `DIWALI20` for 20% off) directly from the dashboard?
- [ ] **Sales Analytics**: Does he want to see a daily/monthly revenue graph and "Best Selling Teas" metrics?

## 5. Branding & Story
Making the website feel authentic.
- [ ] **The "About Us" Story**: What is the origin story of Buddies Cafe? (e.g., How did it start? Why the focus on Nilgiris tea?) We need a good paragraph for the About section.
- [ ] **Social Proof**: Does he have an active Instagram or Google Reviews we can embed on the homepage to build trust?

> [!TIP]
> **Pitching Strategy**
> Start the meeting by pulling up the live prototype on your laptop or phone. Let him click around, add a tea to the cart, and see the smooth animations. Once he is "wowed" by the visuals, transition into asking these logistical questions to show him you are ready to execute!
